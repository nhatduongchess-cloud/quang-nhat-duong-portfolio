Flagship project

CARLA ADAS — Level 3 Active Safety & Autonomous Driving

2026 · Independent · Python / PyTorch / CARLA

An end-to-end autonomous-driving pipeline built in the CARLA simulator: multi-sensor perception → sensor fusion → a committed active-safety arbiter, complemented by a Level-3 ODD/MRM layer and a safety-capped reinforcement-learning cruise controller. Engineered geometry-first and validated through automated testing.

  • Perception: YOLOv8/v10 (FP16), LiDAR clustering (DBSCAN), lane detection and Kalman tracking; LiDAR→camera fusion for true metric range.
  • Calibration-free LiDAR braking corridor — decelerates for any in-path obstacle irrespective of detector output. A missed detection cannot cause a missed brake.
  • Level-3 framework: ODD monitoring and minimal-risk manoeuvre (TOR→MRM→SAFE_STOP); a DQN cruise controller constrained by a hard safety cap.
  • Diagnosed and resolved a collision-inducing defect in the arbiter, then secured it with 179 simulator-free regression checks in GitHub Actions CI.

GuardianCo-Pilot — Guardian AI Operating System

2026 · Team of 5 · Python / YOLOv8 / MediaPipe / Phi-3

An AI Operating System layer above ADAS for VinFast that reads both the road and the driver and makes every warning explainable — running entirely on the edge. It unifies perception, a world model, a planner constrained by a deterministic safety kernel, driver personalization and an in-cabin HMI, closed by a continuous learning loop.

  • Collision-risk monitor: reverse-engineered the organiser's TTC scoring formula, then combined YOLOv8s detection, calibrated SGBM stereo depth, collision-cone gating and tracking — lifting the composite score 19.70 → 65.60 (65.23 leave-one-trip-out).
  • Driver intelligence: ablated four models and shipped an explainable PERCLOS-based rule engine at 84.5 LOTO — outperforming a CNN (18.5) that had overfit to just six drivers' identities.
  • Explainable by design: every decision states its reason in plain language — "microsleep: eyes closed 68% of the last 10 s" — directly addressing driver trust.
  • Edge-deployable: a full submission runs from warm cache in 9 s and on CPU alone (38× GPU speed-up), with no external data or cloud.
